# Market DNA

`MarketDNA` is the persisted feature-vector representation of one normalized pattern.

Important fields include `pattern_window_id`, `normalized_pattern_id`, feature-set identity, source hashes, `configuration_hash`, `feature_vector_hash`, ordered vector values, keyed feature values, availability statuses, diagnostics, and quality flags.

Market DNA is descriptive only. It does not define regimes, outcomes, labels, similarity search, or trading signals.
