# Neighbour outcome dispersion

Neighbour dispersion measures whether nearest neighbours produce more homogeneous outcomes than broad historical baselines.

Metrics include:

- return standard deviation;
- return MAD;
- IQR;
- direction entropy;
- continuation entropy;
- MFE and MAE dispersion.

Low directional skill with lower dispersion may still be useful for uncertainty research, but not as a directional edge.
