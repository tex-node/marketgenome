# Representation quality

A representation is useful only if similarity in representation space organizes some outcome-relevant structure. Step 10A measures:

- feature availability;
- near-constant and unstable features;
- redundancy clusters;
- distance/outcome monotonicity;
- neighbour outcome dispersion;
- episode concentration.

Negative results are valid outcomes. They indicate the current representation may be descriptive but not predictive for the chosen horizon and dataset.
