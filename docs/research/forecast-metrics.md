# Forecast metrics

Validation records directional, probability, return-error, quantile, and distribution-style metrics:

- direction accuracy;
- balanced accuracy;
- Matthews correlation;
- Brier score and Brier skill score;
- log loss;
- MAE and RMSE;
- pinball loss;
- empirical CRPS approximation;
- expected and maximum calibration error.

Metrics are grouped by horizon and method/baseline.
